## How to use this map

- **Definition:** a specification, vocabulary or tool document defines a quantity or behavior. This does not establish its implementation cost or empirical validity.
- **Empirical support:** a study provides findings under its stated conditions. A result from another platform does not supply a browser effect size or repetition count.
- **Methodological precedent:** a source explains a reusable approach. Its assumptions and suitability still need appraisal for this experiment.
- **Study choice:** an operational rule proposed here, informed by the sources. State and justify it explicitly; do not attribute it to an author who did not propose it.

## Evidence for defining lighter and deeper capture

**There is no evidence-based rule that a metric name belongs permanently to a scan tier.** The same LCP value may come from a page observer or from processing a rich trace. Its acquisition path, supporting attribution, lifecycle and cost differ. Define a **capture contract** for each strategy, then measure its cost and diagnostic coverage in the pilot.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Collect a common original-visit record | Performance Timeline `REP5RHB6`; timing/paint/event specifications above; `web-vitals` `N33VTKAN`. | Available primitive signals and attribution mechanisms, subject to coverage and finalization. | Exact collectors, fields, intervals and a measured resource ceiling. Calling this “lightweight” requires pilot evidence. |
| Enable richer tracing or profiling after an alert | Paradyn `TIIDVPV8`; Arnold–Ryder `BUPGR8ZJ`; CDP Profiler `GC9EIJZR`; LMAT `A3SS4UWX`. | Selective/adaptive instrumentation is established; browser mechanisms provide particular outputs. | Trigger, follow-up delay, selected trace categories and evidence-to-fault rules. None guarantees recurrence of the original event. |
| Retain evidence from the original visit | WebREC `ZZSL9IPU`; Performance Timeline `REP5RHB6`; Hindsight `3ED3FDXH`. | Recording the original execution and retrieving bounded evidence differ from rerunning it. | A feasible browser event set, startup time, buffer lifetime, eviction/loss handling and retention cost. No full browser buffer is assumed. |
| Make detailed observations periodically | Adaptive-sampling and cost literature: `BUPGR8ZJ`, `TIIDVPV8`, Mertz–Nunes `WE8HHCA8` and Shahedi et al. `IX2DU87B`. | Sampling and selective tracing have prior art. | Period, randomized phase, complete cost cap and fallback rule. For every candidate capture contract, record **collector/API + event/field set + browser/library version + start/stop rule + sampling/threshold + frame/process scope + buffer/export/retention policy + missingness/loss codes + measured cost**. Keep only contracts that can supply the evidence needed for the selected fault taxonomy within the declared budget. Pilot feasibility, not a blanket metric list, determines the final comparison.

### Study-specific definitions to report explicitly

- **Original regression episode:** an eligible original opportunity in a pilot-validated fault case, confirmed by evaluator-only execution/applicability checks. This is our observation unit, not a quoted universal definition. A shared fault occurrence links related visits.
- **Correct original-cause attribution:** a locked predicted fault class matches the independently verified original class within the deadline and policy budget. This is distinct from alerting on a slow metric or explaining the follow-up visit.
- **Primary attribution rate, diagnostic coverage and conditional diagnostic precision:** use the formulas and denominators in the plan. They are study-specific classification outcomes; metrological measurement precision and numeric agreement are different concepts.
- **Monitoring budget:** a declared vector of resource caps with a complete policy ledger. The chosen caps and weighting are ours. Evaluator-only verification work is reported separately and never supplied to the policy.
- **Reliability boundary:** a condition-dependent empirical conclusion about the tested corpus, using a predefined practical criterion and uncertainty. It is not an existing theorem or an assumed advantage of retention.

The current selection does **not** yet justify a particular confirmatory clustered estimator, powered sample size, minimum useful attribution difference, alert threshold or stopping rule for the main browser experiment. Keep these checks in the [verification queue](<Zotero Verification Queue.md>); obtain and appraise additional methodological sources if needed before adoption.

## Remaining evidence needed before method adoption

1. **Capture contracts:** confirm exact events and attribution fields on the pinned build, including loss and lifecycle behavior; measure cost before naming a contract lightweight or detailed.
2. **Cause labels:** validate that each injected change executes and produces the intended degradation in controlled pilot comparisons. Keep eligibility independent of strategy alerts and measured slow values.
3. **Inference:** obtain and appraise a method suited to the actual site/fault/session hierarchy and number of independent clusters. An abstract-only bootstrap or agreement citation is insufficient.
4. **Protocol choices:** justify cadence, corpus size, severity/persistence, delays, resource caps, practical-effect criterion, splitting, stopping and missingness rules. Sources inform these choices; they do not prescribe them.

Track these tasks in the [verification queue](<Zotero Verification Queue.md>). The study can make an empirical contribution even if delayed diagnosis performs well; novelty and superiority remain questions for evidence.

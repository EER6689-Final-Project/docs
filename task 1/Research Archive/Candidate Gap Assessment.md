### Decision rules

- **Evidence** is what an original source actually states or reports; an **inference** is a question prompted by juxtaposing sources. Absence of a paper in this collection or a short search result is *not* evidence of a literature gap.
- Assess each area for (1) a clear measurand and comparator, (2) evidence of a practical problem, (3) known overlapping methods, (4) feasibility within [[../My tasks.pdf|individual task allocation]], (5) falsifiability and (6) unresolved source/requirements gates. No weighted ranking is justified until the required contribution and evaluation budget are approved.
- A browser instrument agreeing with Lighthouse or DevTools tests compatibility under specified conditions; it does not by itself establish construct validity, user-perceived QoE, causal attribution, novelty, or a universal score.

### Historical cross-cutting contradictions and scope questions

The following requests are retained as historical 2026-10-08 wording. They are **not current open approval requests**: bounded planning choices were subsequently recorded in [Scope Resolution](<Scope Resolution.md>). Exact technical contracts, methods, resource limits and permissions remain open as described above.
1. **Task document calls FCP, TBT, Speed Index, TTI and “server response time” Core Web Vitals and omits INP.** This is a *requirements/terminology conflict*, not a licence to delete requested features. Official current set is LCP/INP/CLS (`UN6NKBQX`, `VWHJLGV3`); TTI is historical, TBT is a lab diagnostic, and client TTFB is not server-internal time (`DP9EINBS`). **Approval needed:** preserve requested diagnostics as non-CWV; clarify whether scripted INP and legacy TTI are required and how “server response time” should be named without misrepresenting TTFB.
2. **“Accurate compared with DevTools/Lighthouse” has no unique unit or ground truth.** A matched Chromium metric may agree numerically while a different API scope, trace window, device profile or score version yields a valid disagreement. **Approval needed:** which metrics are required for numerical agreement and which merely for documented differences?
3. **The required transparent score need not mean a universal subjective-experience scalar.** Existing v10 Lighthouse weights are not a current, transferable validation of custom weights (`WB6JZEUA`). **Approval needed:** intended decision user/purpose and whether a multi-dimensional severity summary is acceptable while preserving raw components.
4. **Static/image-heavy/JavaScript-heavy/poorly optimized** are required testing categories, but should be defined independently of the profiler under test. Phase C does not define their thresholds or sample counts; these depend on the selected estimand and protocol.

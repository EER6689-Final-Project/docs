# T1.1 Candidate Gap Assessment — Phase C

**Status:** Comparative assessment of candidate problem areas, not a selected thesis gap or final research design. **Date:** 2026-10-08. **Scope:** The eight original papers and twelve non-paper records in Zotero `VEYKC53A`, with a *limited overlap scan* of adjacent Zotero records and one official Chrome soft-navigation page. This assessment does not complete Phase 0 or verify all T1.2–T1.6 claims.

## Decision rules

- **Evidence** is what an original source actually states or reports; an **inference** is a question prompted by juxtaposing sources. Absence of a paper in this collection or a short search result is *not* evidence of a literature gap.
- Assess each area for (1) a clear measurand and comparator, (2) evidence of a practical problem, (3) known overlapping methods, (4) feasibility within [[../My tasks.pdf|individual task allocation]], (5) falsifiability and (6) unresolved source/requirements gates. No weighted ranking is justified until the required contribution and evaluation budget are approved.
- A browser instrument agreeing with Lighthouse or DevTools tests compatibility under specified conditions; it does not by itself establish construct validity, user-perceived QoE, causal attribution, novelty, or a universal score.

## Cross-cutting contradictions and scope decisions to request

1. **Task document calls FCP, TBT, Speed Index, TTI and “server response time” Core Web Vitals and omits INP.** This is a *requirements/terminology conflict*, not a licence to delete requested features. Official current set is LCP/INP/CLS (`UN6NKBQX`, `VWHJLGV3`); TTI is historical, TBT is a lab diagnostic, and client TTFB is not server-internal time (`DP9EINBS`). **Approval needed:** preserve requested diagnostics as non-CWV; clarify whether scripted INP and legacy TTI are required and how “server response time” should be named without misrepresenting TTFB.
2. **“Accurate compared with DevTools/Lighthouse” has no unique unit or ground truth.** A matched Chromium metric may agree numerically while a different API scope, trace window, device profile or score version yields a valid disagreement. **Approval needed:** which metrics are required for numerical agreement and which merely for documented differences?
3. **The required transparent score need not mean a universal subjective-experience scalar.** Existing v10 Lighthouse weights are not a current, transferable validation of custom weights (`WB6JZEUA`). **Approval needed:** intended decision user/purpose and whether a multi-dimensional severity summary is acceptable while preserving raw components.
4. **Static/image-heavy/JavaScript-heavy/poorly optimized** are required testing categories, but should be defined independently of the profiler under test. Phase C does not define their thresholds or sample counts; these depend on the selected estimand and protocol.

## Limited overlap scan — provenance and what it does *not* prove

- Zotero local semantic searches on 8 Oct 2026 for `browser performance profiler measurement validation Lighthouse DevTools metric agreement repeatability`, `web vitals synthetic interaction INP SPA soft navigation lifecycle measurement`, and `performance profiling metric semantics versioned score measurement provenance browser instrumentation` returned overlapping records outside T1.1. This is **discovery**, not systematic review or evidence of absence. The semantic database reported 384 documents (last updated 7 Oct 2026).
- `DJI5ULJD` — Tjaša Heričko, Boštjan Šumak & Saša Brdnik, *Towards representative web performance measurements with Google Lighthouse*: original Zotero full text's abstract reports run variability and the conditional five-run median; Zotero metadata is poor (ResearchGate item with no DOI/creators), so locate bibliographic original and read methods/results before citing its recommendation.
- `RB93JMBB` — Demir et al. (2022), *Reproducibility and Replicability of Web Measurement Studies*, DOI [10.1145/3485447.3512214](https://doi.org/10.1145/3485447.3512214): abstract reports a 117-paper survey and 4.5-million-page/24-setup test. Only partial original text was inspected; **method-level verification pending**.
- `MAYMCK7L` — Zotero record metadata lacks date and authors beyond Hantke/Stock, while attached USENIX Security '25 PDF names *Florian Hantke, Peter Snyder, Hamed Haddadi and Ben Stock*. Abstract presents WebREC/.web for accurate reproducible general browser measurement; **scope/methods not yet fully read**. Do not use the incomplete Zotero metadata as a final citation.
- `486KI7NN` — [Chrome soft-navigation documentation](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/web-platform/soft-navigations), retrieved 8 Oct 2026: active evolving solution undercuts a blanket “SPAs cannot be measured” claim. Page references Chrome 151 availability; check release timing and flags rather than assuming present stable support.
- Web-search integration returned **cancelled** for targeted external searches; Phase C therefore **does not claim comprehensive 2026 literature coverage**. Search other databases, official releases and citation chains before any novelty claim.

## What Phase C resolves—and does not

- **Resolved:** the four areas are differentiated by measurand, evaluable contrast, closest known overlap and decision gates; the requirements conflict is explicit. The twenty T1.1 records support *relevance* of conditional measurement semantics but no universal, unstudied literature gap.
- **Not resolved:** final thesis gap, question, hypothesis, aims, methodology, sample size, metric set, score, software architecture, acceptance criteria or Phase 0 exit. Continue primary-source verification in T1.2–T1.6 and overlap reading before selection.

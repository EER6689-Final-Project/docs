## Research question and approach

The abstract considers approximate confidence intervals for a parameter in a multiparameter family. It describes bootstrap intervals that incorporate transformations and bias corrections, including for nonparametric situations, with the goal of improved approximation accuracy. It notes increased computational effort as the cost (abstract; DOI/publisher record).

## Findings from the source

The abstract reports that the proposed interval methods achieve second-order correctness across a wide variety of problems. It frames bootstrap intervals as a way to incorporate corrections that are often applied manually to standard approximate intervals (abstract).

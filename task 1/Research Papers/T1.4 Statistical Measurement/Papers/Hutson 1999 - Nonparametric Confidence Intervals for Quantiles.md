## Research question and approach

Hutson presents an algorithm for constructing a preselected (100(1-\alpha)\)% nonparametric confidence interval for an arbitrary quantile, including the median or quartiles. The method approximates the distribution of a linear interpolation quantile estimator using a fractional order statistic (abstract).

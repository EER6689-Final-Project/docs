# JCGM VIM3 §2.18 — Measurement Bias

- **Citation:** Joint Committee for Guides in Metrology (JCGM), *International Vocabulary of Metrology — Basic and General Concepts and Associated Terms (VIM3)*, entry 2.18, “measurement bias.” [Current entry](https://jcgm.bipm.org/vim/en/2.18.html).
- **Zotero key:** `4MGN3SYQ` (webpage; imported HTML snapshot attached).
- **Source access:** Online VIM3 entry and Zotero snapshot checked 13 October 2026.

## Outline Summary

- **Definition:** Measurement bias is an estimate of systematic measurement error.
- **Project relevance:** A persistent difference between profiler and reference outputs may indicate bias only after the measurand, conditions, and comparison method are aligned; the observed difference alone does not identify its cause.
- **Limit:** The vocabulary entry defines the term but does not provide an estimator, test, or browser-specific example.

## T1.5 Evidence Check

- **Source-supported:** The concise VIM3 definition.
- **Researcher inference:** Separate systematic offsets from run-to-run spread when analyzing matched profiler/reference measurements.

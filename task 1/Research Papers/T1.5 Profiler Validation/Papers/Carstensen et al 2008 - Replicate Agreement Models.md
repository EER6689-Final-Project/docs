# Statistical models for assessing agreement in method comparison studies with replicate measurements

- **Citation:** Carstensen, B., Simpson, J., & Gurrin, L. C. (2008). Statistical models for assessing agreement in method comparison studies with replicate measurements. *The International Journal of Biostatistics, 4*(1), Article 16. https://doi.org/10.2202/1557-4679.1107
- **Zotero key:** `6VK2G66S`
- **Source available in Zotero:** PubMed record only; no paper PDF attached. This note is based on the Zotero bibliographic record and abstract.

## Scope and contribution

The authors discuss the statistical model underlying classical limits of agreement and extend it to method-comparison data with replicate measurements. The abstract says the paper supplies computer code for fitting the models and demonstrates deriving repeatability measures and limits of agreement from the model output.

## Relevance to profiler validation

Repeated runs on the same page or environment are clustered observations, not automatically independent experimental units. This source is directly relevant to estimating within-method repeatability and between-method agreement while representing replicate structure.

## Evidence boundary and limitations

The abstract does not identify the worked example's sample size, number or allocation of replicates, exact model specification, or code environment. The method requires an appropriate model for the replication and dependence structure; the available record is insufficient to reproduce the analysis or judge its assumptions. The paper is methodological and does not validate browser profilers.

# JCGM VIM3 §2.25 — Measurement Reproducibility

- **Citation:** Joint Committee for Guides in Metrology (JCGM), *International Vocabulary of Metrology — Basic and General Concepts and Associated Terms (VIM3)*, entry 2.25, “measurement reproducibility.” [Current entry](https://jcgm.bipm.org/vim/en/2.25.html).
- **Zotero key:** `98WM2BIA` (webpage; no child attachment).
- **Source access:** Online VIM3 entry checked 13 October 2026.

## Outline Summary

- **Definition:** Reproducibility is measurement precision under reproducibility conditions; the entry points to ISO 5725-1 and ISO 5725-2 for related statistical terms.
- **Project relevance:** Reproduction across machines, browser versions, or sites is a different condition set from repeatability on one fixed setup; reports should say which changes are permitted.
- **Limit:** This vocabulary entry does not prescribe the project’s replication design or a numerical reproducibility criterion.

## T1.5 Evidence Check

- **Source-supported:** The definition and reference to ISO 5725 terminology.
- **Researcher inference:** Do not use repeatability and reproducibility interchangeably when comparing profiler runs across environments.

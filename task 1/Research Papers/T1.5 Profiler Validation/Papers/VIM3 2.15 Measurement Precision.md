# JCGM VIM3 §2.15 — Measurement Precision

- **Citation:** Joint Committee for Guides in Metrology (JCGM), *International Vocabulary of Metrology — Basic and General Concepts and Associated Terms (VIM3)*, entry 2.15, “measurement precision.” [Current entry](https://jcgm.bipm.org/vim/en/2.15.html).
- **Zotero key:** `DT5MQ99J` (webpage; no child attachment).
- **Source access:** Online VIM3 entry checked 13 October 2026.

## Outline Summary

- **Definition:** Precision is the closeness of agreement among replicate measurements on the same or similar objects under specified conditions.
- **Expression:** VIM notes that precision is usually expressed through imprecision measures such as standard deviation, variance, or coefficient of variation under those conditions.
- **Conditions:** Repeatability, intermediate-precision, and reproducibility conditions are distinct specified conditions; precision helps define each.
- **Project relevance:** A repeat-run spread is interpretable only with browser, page, instrumentation, cache, and run conditions made explicit. Precision does not establish closeness to a true value.
- **Limit:** VIM3 is terminology guidance, not a web measurement study or a recommendation for a particular dispersion statistic.

## T1.5 Evidence Check

- **Source-supported:** The definition and listed measures/conditions.
- **Researcher inference:** Report the conditions before interpreting repeat-run variability as profiler precision.

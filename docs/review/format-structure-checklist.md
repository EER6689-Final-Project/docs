# Format and structure verification checklist

## Authority, scope, and how to record a result

The **two-page supplied [`format.pdf`](../format.pdf)** is the primary authority for formatting. Its page 1 has the “Arrangement of Report” list and checklist rows 1–4; page 2 has rows 5–13 and the candidate signature/date area. The project-specific instructions below override only identified guide items or supply a document sequence where the guide says some items are optional. Do not treat the currently available `docs/main.pdf` as a final proof: it predates the current manuscript revision. Every rendered-PDF check below applies to a **fresh build after those revisions**, including the `preserve-format-pages.py` post-processing step.

For each ID, the final verifier should record **PASS / FAIL / N/A**, the source location inspected, the physical PDF page(s) inspected, and evidence or a precise defect. A source declaration alone is insufficient for visual or PDF-metric checks. An unused template file does not establish that a component is in the compiled report; inspect `main.tex` and the built PDF. The guide uses “leftJustified” for body and references; check left alignment without silently inventing a full-justification requirement. The guide does not set the page size for authored pages; this project uses A4, while the two supplied pages must retain their original US Letter geometry.

## Guide ambiguities that must not become invented requirements

- The guide does not prescribe A4 or another size for authored pages. Its own two original pages are Letter (612 × 792 PDF points); retain that exact size at physical pages 3–4.
- “LeftJustified” does not unambiguously require a flush right edge. Test a consistent left edge and readable rag; do not fail a report solely for ragged-right text.
- The guide gives `1.0 1.1 1.1.1` as section-number examples, not a demand to create a `1.0` section or every level in every chapter.
- The guide says some arrangement items may be optional. The user's 13-component order and explicit omissions determine this report's actual components.
- `format.pdf` is a supplied checklist artifact whose boxes and signature area must remain blank. Its fonts, preprinted page furniture, and Letter geometry are **source content**, not authored-report formatting defects.

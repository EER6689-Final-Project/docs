\section{Basis and Dependencies}

This is a prospective sequence, not a completion claim or fixed calendar. G3/G4 (closest-source/contribution readiness), G5 (measurement contracts), G6 (pilot execution readiness) and G7 (main-study readiness) remain open. FP-0.1 is proposed and unrun; its two fixtures, 652 planned/700 maximum starts, eight active hours, seven-day duration and 8-GiB campaign cap are limits for feasibility work, not standards, power calculations or main-study defaults \parencite{3ED3FDXH,GJMALCNF,B9FIJAKE}.

Phases follow a relative sequence. The person-day ranges are planning assumptions, not verified commitments. Calendar duration depends on host/tool readiness and review. No collection begins before its execution manifest and relevant readiness checks are complete.

\section{Roles, Review, Change Control and Risks}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Luna explores and drafts:} map evidence and limitations to O1--O5, propose prose/tables and identify open claims. Do not change the research question or convert recommendations into source findings.
\item \textbf{Sol synthesizes:} reconcile overlapping text, identifiers, status, costs and denominators into one manuscript; preserve FP-0.1 versus main-study boundaries.
\item \textbf{A different Sol reviews:} before build, check source versions, episode oracle, C--B comparison, all-attempt denominator, costs, novelty language, citations and limitations. Return substantive corrections and freeze a reviewable snapshot.
\item \textbf{A separate Sol build agent:} only after review, integrate sources, check BibLaTeX keys, compile XeLaTeX/Biber, inspect logs and rendered pages, and repair layout. It must return scientific changes to review rather than silently changing claims.
\end{enumerate}

FP-0.1 permits one documented pre-comparison repair cycle in its 48-start reserve, with a bounded severity increase only if qualification fails. A new fixture, fault class, detector, capture method or larger budget requires prospective FP-0.2. During comparison, arm-specific failure is an outcome; wrong linkage, label leakage or shared integrity failure stops the campaign. Any main-protocol change requires a versioned amendment and renewed review before affected collection. Green pilot progression means only “prepare a main-study protocol.”

\paragraph{Package acceptance.} All required components appear in order; every caption citation resolves to a Zotero parent key in \texttt{docs/references.bib}; no visible drafting prompt or unrun result remains. Check references, lists, numbering, margins, font, metadata and rendered pages. Successful compilation alone does not establish visual compliance. No study collection, Zotero mutation or main-study authorization is part of this writing plan.
